About me

I'm a Software Engineering student with a background in physics and astronomy and a growing interest in building software. I enjoy understanding how things work, breaking complicated problems into manageable pieces, and experimenting with technology to find practical solutions. My path so far has taken me through science, data, sustainability, and programming — and I'm now bringing these experiences together in software development.

Think Like a Physicist – build Like an Engineer

My background combines physics, astronomy, data, and software engineering. I started out wanting to understand how the universe works, which led me to study physics and astronomy at Aarhus University. Along the way, mathematics, programming, and data analysis became an important part of my work. I found that I particularly enjoyed the process of taking a complex problem, breaking it down, and finding a way to make sense of it.

From Scientific Problems to Software

Programming originally entered my life as a tool for solving scientific problems. I used it for analysing data, mathematical modelling, simulations, and scientific research. Over time, however, I became increasingly interested in programming itself. I wanted to move beyond using code as a tool and learn how to design and build software. That curiosity eventually led me to Software Engineering, where I am now developing a deeper understanding of software development and exploring how technology can be used to create practical solutions.

Learning Through Experience

I learn best by getting my hands dirty and actually building things. My studies, internships, courses, and personal projects have given me the opportunity to explore different technologies and approaches, from data and sustainability to web development and software engineering. I enjoy the process of starting with an idea, figuring out how to turn it into something functional, and learning from the problems that inevitably appear along the way.

Beyond the Screen

There is more to me than technology. I enjoy spending time outdoors, especially camping, and I like activities that give me a chance to slow down and work with something tangible. I play piano, enjoy cross-stitching, and value spending time with my family. For me, having interests outside technology is just as important as continuously learning within it.
